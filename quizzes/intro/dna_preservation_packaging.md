# 🧪 DNA Preservation & Packaging

### 84. Contamination during DNA collection may occur through:
A) Proper labeling  
B) Coughing over evidence  
C) Wearing gloves  
D) Refrigeration  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Coughing over evidence**  
*Explanation: Contamination happens when foreign DNA or other material contacts the sample, such as from saliva in a cough.*
</details>

---

### 85. Evidence collectors should wear protective gear to:
A) Look professional  
B) Avoid contamination  
C) Speed collection  
D) Reduce paperwork  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Avoid contamination**  
*Explanation: Gloves, masks, and other protective equipment prevent the collector's DNA or other materials from contaminating evidence.*
</details>

---

### 86. Wet biological evidence should ideally be:
A) Sealed immediately in plastic  
B) Thrown away  
C) Dried before packaging  
D) Frozen instantly  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Dried before packaging**  
*Explanation: Wet biological samples must be dried to prevent bacterial growth and DNA degradation before packaging.*
</details>

---

### 87. Porous packaging materials such as paper bags are used to:
A) Increase moisture  
B) Prevent degradation  
C) Destroy bacteria  
D) Hide evidence  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Prevent degradation**  
*Explanation: Paper allows air circulation, preventing mold and preserving DNA, unlike plastic which traps moisture.*
</details>

---

### 88. Evidence items should be packaged:
A) Together  
B) Separately  
C) In plastic only  
D) Without labels  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Separately**  
*Explanation: Packaging evidence separately prevents cross-contamination between samples.*
</details>

---

### 89. Evidence must be marked with:
A) Officer rank  
B) Case theory  
C) Collector’s initials and date  
D) Suspect’s name  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Collector’s initials and date**  
*Explanation: Labeling with the collector’s information maintains the chain of custody and accountability for the evidence.*
</details>
