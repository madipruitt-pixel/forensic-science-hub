# 🔗 Chain of Custody

Welcome to the **Chain of Custody** quiz! Click on each question to reveal the answer and a brief explanation.

---

### 90. The chain of custody ensures:
A) Faster convictions  
B) Evidence integrity  
C) Public transparency  
D) Media access  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Evidence integrity**  
*Explanation: Maintaining a proper chain of custody ensures evidence is authentic and untampered from collection to court.*
</details>

---

### 91. Every individual who handles evidence must be:
A) Approved by a judge  
B) Documented  
C) Photographed  
D) Interviewed  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Documented**  
*Explanation: Logging all personnel who handle evidence creates accountability and preserves its integrity.*
</details>

---

### 92. Failure to maintain chain of custody may result in:
A) Stronger cases  
B) Evidence exclusion  
C) Faster trials  
D) Higher sentences  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Evidence exclusion**  
*Explanation: Courts may reject evidence if the chain of custody is broken, compromising the case.*
</details>

---

### 93. A chain-of-custody record must include:
A) Weather conditions  
B) Collector’s initials and date  
C) Jury opinion  
D) Suspect’s confession  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Collector’s initials and date**  
*Explanation: Each entry in the chain-of-custody record must identify who handled the evidence and when.*
</details>

---

### 94. Standard/reference samples are used to:
A) Replace evidence  
B) Compare unknown evidence to known sources  
C) Destroy contamination  
D) Identify judges  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Compare unknown evidence to known sources**  
*Explanation: Reference samples provide a basis for comparison to confirm the identity of evidence.*
</details>
