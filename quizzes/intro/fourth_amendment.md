# ⚖️ Fourth Amendment & Legal Considerations

Welcome to the **Fourth Amendment & Legal Considerations** quiz! Click on each question to reveal the answer and a brief explanation.

---

### 95. The Fourth Amendment protects against:
A) Self-incrimination  
B) Double jeopardy  
C) Unreasonable searches and seizures  
D) Cruel punishment  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Unreasonable searches and seizures**  
*Explanation: The Fourth Amendment limits government power, requiring lawful justification for searches and seizures.*
</details>

---

### 96. Evidence collected in violation of the Fourth Amendment may be:
A) Enhanced  
B) Automatically accepted  
C) Excluded from court  
D) Strengthened  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Excluded from court**  
*Explanation: The exclusionary rule prevents illegally obtained evidence from being used in trials.*
</details>

---

### 97. Warrantless searches are permitted under:
A) No circumstances  
B) Emergency situations  
C) Only federal cases  
D) Media pressure  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Emergency situations**  
*Explanation: Exceptions to the warrant requirement include exigent circumstances where safety or evidence is at risk.*
</details>

---

### 98. Consent is one example of:
A) Illegal search  
B) Warrantless search exception  
C) Chain-of-custody break  
D) Court appeal  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Warrantless search exception**  
*Explanation: If an individual voluntarily consents, law enforcement may conduct a search without a warrant.*
</details>

---

### 99. The Supreme Court emphasizes obtaining a warrant when:
A) It is convenient  
B) Time allows  
C) Media requests it  
D) Officers prefer it  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Time allows**  
*Explanation: Courts generally require warrants unless urgent circumstances prevent it.*
</details>

---

### 100. Proper evidence collection must comply with constitutional standards to:
A) Avoid paperwork  
B) Ensure admissibility in court  
C) Reduce investigation time  
D) Increase arrest rates  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Ensure admissibility in court**  
*Explanation: Following constitutional procedures preserves the legal validity of collected evidence.*
</details>
