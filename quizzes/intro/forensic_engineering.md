### 49. Forensic engineers typically investigate:
A) Handwriting  
B) Accidents and structural failures  
C) Behavioral disorders  
D) DNA sequencing  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Accidents and structural failures**  
*Explanation: Forensic engineers analyze structures, materials, and accidents to determine causes and assign responsibility.*
</details>

---

### 50. A forensic engineer may analyze:
A) Mechanical systems  
B) Blood spatter  
C) Dental impressions  
D) Fingerprints  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**A) Mechanical systems**  
*Explanation: They study mechanical or structural components that may have contributed to an accident or failure.*
</details>

---

### 51. Determining responsibility for structural collapse is part of:
A) Criminalistics  
B) Forensic psychiatry  
C) Forensic engineering  
D) Toxicology  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Forensic engineering**  
*Explanation: Assessing the causes of structural collapse or material failure falls within forensic engineering.*
</details>
