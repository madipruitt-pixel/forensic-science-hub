### 129. IAFIS stands for:
A) Integrated Analysis of Firearm Identification System  
B) Integrated Automated Fingerprint Identification System  
C) International Automated Fingerprint Index System  
D) Identification and Analysis of Forensic Imaging System  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Integrated Automated Fingerprint Identification System**  
*Explanation: IAFIS is the FBI’s national fingerprint database for identifying suspects.*
</details>

---

### 130. IAFIS is maintained by the:
A) DEA  
B) CIA  
C) FBI  
D) ATF  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) FBI**  
*Explanation: The Federal Bureau of Investigation operates IAFIS to store and match fingerprints.*
</details>

---

### 131. CODIS is used for:
A) Fingerprints  
B) Ballistics  
C) DNA profiling  
D) Shoeprints  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) DNA profiling**  
*Explanation: CODIS (Combined DNA Index System) links DNA profiles from crime scenes and offenders.*
</details>

---

### 132. NIBIN is primarily associated with:
A) Paint analysis  
B) Firearm analysis  
C) DNA databases  
D) Fibers  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Firearm analysis**  
*Explanation: NIBIN (National Integrated Ballistic Information Network) stores ballistic signatures to link firearms to crimes.*
</details>

---

### 133. PDQ is a database used for:
A) DNA  
B) Automotive paint information  
C) Shoeprint impressions  
D) Explosives  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Automotive paint information**  
*Explanation: PDQ contains chemical and color profiles of car paints to help identify vehicle involvement in crimes.*
</details>

---

### 134. SICAR assists in analyzing:
A) Shoeprints  
B) Fingerprints  
C) Blood spatter  
D) Tool marks  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**A) Shoeprints**  
*Explanation: SICAR (Shoeprint Image Capture and Retrieval) helps investigators compare shoe impressions.*
</details>

---

### 135. Which system links firearm evidence across crimes?
A) CODIS  
B) NIBIN  
C) PDQ  
D) SICAR  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) NIBIN**  
*Explanation: NIBIN allows investigators to connect ballistic evidence from different cases using firearm signatures.*
</details>
