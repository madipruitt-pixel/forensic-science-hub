# 🧠 Forensic Psychiatry

### 41. Forensic psychiatry focuses on the relationship between:
A) Evidence and laboratories  
B) Human behavior and the legal system  
C) Fingerprints and identification  
D) Engineering and crime scenes  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Human behavior and the legal system**  
*Explanation: Forensic psychiatry applies psychiatric knowledge to legal issues, including criminal and civil cases.*
</details>

---

### 42. In criminal cases, forensic psychiatrists may evaluate:
A) Blood evidence  
B) Structural damage  
C) Behavioral disorders  
D) Handwriting  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Behavioral disorders**  
*Explanation: They assess mental health and behavior relevant to a defendant’s criminal responsibility or competency.*
</details>

---

### 43. In civil cases, forensic psychiatrists often assess:
A) Ballistics  
B) Competency  
C) Toxicology  
D) DNA profiles  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Competency**  
*Explanation: Forensic psychiatrists evaluate a person’s capacity to make decisions or participate in legal proceedings.*
</details>

---

### 44. Behavioral profiling is used to:
A) Identify blood type  
B) Predict suspect characteristics  
C) Determine cause of death  
D) Classify fingerprints  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Predict suspect characteristics**  
*Explanation: Behavioral profiling analyzes crime patterns to infer characteristics of an unknown offender.*
</details>
