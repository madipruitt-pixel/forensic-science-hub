### 55. A polygraph measures:
A) DNA structure  
B) Physiological responses  
C) Fingerprint ridges  
D) Blood alcohol levels  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Physiological responses**  
*Explanation: Polygraphs detect changes in heart rate, blood pressure, respiration, and skin conductivity that may indicate stress or deception.*
</details>

---

### 56. The reliability of polygraph results in court is:
A) Universally accepted  
B) Rarely questioned  
C) Often debated  
D) Guaranteed accurate  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Often debated**  
*Explanation: Courts question polygraph accuracy due to variability in individual physiological responses and potential for error.*
</details>

### 57. Voiceprint analysis is most useful in cases involving:
A) Written notes  
B) Recorded conversations  
C) Blood samples  
D) Structural fires  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Recorded conversations**  
*Explanation: Voiceprint analysis compares characteristics of speech in recordings to identify or verify speakers.*
</details>
