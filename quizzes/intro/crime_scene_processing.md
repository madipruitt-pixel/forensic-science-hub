# 🚨 Crime Scene Processing & First Responders

### 61. Forensic science begins primarily at the:
A) Laboratory  
B) Police station  
C) Crime scene  
D) Courtroom  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Crime scene**  
*Explanation: The crime scene is the initial location where evidence is recognized and preserved, forming the foundation for investigation.*
</details>

---

### 62. Proper recognition and preservation of evidence is important because it:
A) Speeds up trials  
B) Ensures evidence integrity  
C) Reduces paperwork  
D) Guarantees conviction  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Ensures evidence integrity**  
*Explanation: Maintaining evidence integrity prevents contamination or loss, which is crucial for admissibility in court.*
</details>

---

### 63. The first responding officer must immediately:
A) Begin laboratory testing  
B) Secure and protect the scene  
C) Interview neighbors  
D) Collect all evidence  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Secure and protect the scene**  
*Explanation: Securing the scene prevents unauthorized access and preserves evidence for investigation.*
</details>

---

### 64. The first priority upon arrival at a violent crime scene is:
A) Photographing evidence  
B) Sketching the scene  
C) Providing medical assistance  
D) Writing the final report  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Providing medical assistance**  
*Explanation: Officer safety and attending to any victims is the immediate priority before evidence processing.*
</details>

---

### 65. Unauthorized personnel must be excluded primarily to prevent:
A) Media interference  
B) Contamination  
C) Delays  
D) Paperwork errors  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Contamination**  
*Explanation: Limiting access reduces the risk of altering or contaminating evidence.*
</details>

---

### 66. A log of individuals entering and exiting the scene is maintained to:
A) Track contamination risks  
B) Impress supervisors  
C) Count witnesses  
D) Monitor crowd size  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**A) Track contamination risks**  
*Explanation: Maintaining a log ensures accountability and identifies potential sources of contamination.*
</details>

---

### 67. Officers should avoid smoking or moving objects at a scene because it may:
A) Waste time  
B) Alter evidence  
C) Delay arrest  
D) Violate department policy only  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Alter evidence**  
*Explanation: Any disturbance at the scene can compromise the integrity of the evidence.*
</details>

---

### 68. Environmental conditions at a scene should remain unchanged to preserve:
A) Temperature comfort  
B) Officer safety  
C) Evidence integrity  
D) Lighting quality  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Evidence integrity**  
*Explanation: Changes in temperature, moisture, or light can degrade or destroy physical evidence.*
</details>
