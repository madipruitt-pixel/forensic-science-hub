### 74. The strip/line search pattern is best used for:
A) Circular rooms  
B) Large outdoor areas with clear boundaries  
C) Small closets  
D) Multi-level buildings  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Large outdoor areas with clear boundaries**  
*Explanation: Line searches allow systematic coverage of large open areas and are efficient for outdoor crime scenes.*
</details>

---

### 75. The grid search pattern increases thoroughness by:
A) Using only one investigator  
B) Searching from the center outward  
C) Performing two line searches  
D) Dividing into zones  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Performing two line searches**  
*Explanation: A grid search involves two perpendicular line searches over the same area, improving detection of evidence.*
</details>

---

### 76. The spiral search pattern is often used for locating:
A) DNA  
B) Footprints  
C) Tool marks  
D) Firearms  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Footprints**  
*Explanation: Spiral searches allow investigators to move inwards or outwards, covering open areas effectively, often used for trace evidence like footprints.*
</details>

---

### 77. A weakness of the wheel/ray pattern is that:
A) It requires too many officers  
B) It misses areas between rays  
C) It is too slow  
D) It damages evidence  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) It misses areas between rays**  
*Explanation: The ray pattern focuses on a central point, so spaces between rays may be overlooked if not carefully covered.*
</details>

---

### 78. The quadrant/zone pattern is ideal for:
A) Very small scenes  
B) Single-room crimes  
C) Large or complex areas  
D) Vehicle stops  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Large or complex areas**  
*Explanation: Dividing a scene into zones allows multiple investigators to search thoroughly without missing evidence in complicated layouts.*
</details>
