### 45. Forensic odontology primarily involves the study of:
A) Bones  
B) Blood  
C) Teeth  
D) Fibers  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Teeth**  
*Explanation: Forensic odontologists analyze teeth to assist in identification, bite mark analysis, and age estimation.*
</details>

---

### 46. Teeth are valuable for identification because they:
A) Change frequently  
B) Are easily destroyed  
C) Are highly resilient  
D) Contain DNA in all cases  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Are highly resilient**  
*Explanation: Teeth can survive decomposition, fire, or trauma, making them reliable for identification.*
</details>

---

### 47. Bite mark analysis may be used to:
A) Determine blood type  
B) Match dental patterns to a suspect  
C) Identify fingerprints  
D) Analyze gunshot residue  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Match dental patterns to a suspect**  
*Explanation: Bite marks left on victims or objects can be compared to a suspect’s dental profile.*
</details>

---

### 48. Dental records are especially useful when:
A) The suspect confesses  
B) The body is unrecognizable  
C) DNA is unavailable  
D) Eyewitnesses disagree  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) The body is unrecognizable**  
*Explanation: Dental records allow identification when visual recognition or fingerprints are not possible.*
</details>
