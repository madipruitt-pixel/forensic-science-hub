### 52. Digital forensic analysts commonly recover:
A) Fingerprints  
B) Deleted electronic data  
C) Bone fragments  
D) Fiber samples  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Deleted electronic data**  
*Explanation: Digital forensic analysts specialize in recovering, preserving, and analyzing data from computers and electronic devices, including deleted files.*
</details>

### 53. Tracking hacking activity falls under:
A) Toxicology  
B) Forensic odontology  
C) Digital forensics  
D) Forensic anthropology  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Digital forensics**  
*Explanation: Digital forensics includes investigating cybercrimes, such as hacking, malware attacks, and unauthorized data access.*
</details>

### 54. The importance of digital evidence is increasing because:
A) Crime scenes no longer exist  
B) Most communication involves digital devices  
C) Fingerprints are outdated  
D) Courts prefer digital data  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Most communication involves digital devices**  
*Explanation: With widespread use of computers, smartphones, and the internet, digital evidence is increasingly critical in investigations.*
</details>
