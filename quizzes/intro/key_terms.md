# 🔬 Basic Definitions & Core Concepts

### 1. Forensic science is best defined as:
A) The study of crime scenes  
B) The application of science to law  
C) The study of criminal behavior  
D) The analysis of suspects  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) The application of science to law**  
*Explanation: Forensic science applies scientific principles and techniques to legal questions and cases.*
</details>

---

### 2. Criminalistics focuses primarily on:
A) Interrogating suspects  
B) Courtroom procedures  
C) Physical evidence analysis  
D) Criminal profiling  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Physical evidence analysis**  
*Explanation: Criminalistics is the branch of forensic science concerned with identifying and analyzing physical evidence.*
</details>

---

### 3. The CSI Effect refers to:
A) Increased crime rates  
B) Media exaggeration of forensic science  
C) Use of digital evidence  
D) Police procedural reforms  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Media exaggeration of forensic science**  
*Explanation: The CSI Effect describes how crime TV shows create unrealistic public expectations about forensic evidence in court.*
</details>

---

### 4. Locard’s Exchange Principle states that:
A) Evidence must be preserved immediately  
B) Every crime scene contains DNA  
C) Contact between objects results in material exchange  
D) Crime scenes are always contaminated  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Contact between objects results in material exchange**  
*Explanation: Locard's Principle states that every contact leaves a trace, meaning materials are exchanged when two objects touch.*
</details>

---

### 5. Which of the following is an example of physical evidence?
A) A confession  
B) A witness statement  
C) A fingerprint  
D) A rumor  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) A fingerprint**  
*Explanation: Physical evidence is tangible material that can be observed and analyzed, such as fingerprints or hair.*
</details>

---

### 6. Physical evidence is often considered more reliable than eyewitness testimony because it is:
A) Emotional  
B) Objective  
C) Opinion-based  
D) Voluntary  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Objective**  
*Explanation: Physical evidence is factual and measurable, unlike eyewitness testimony which can be influenced by perception and memory.*
</details>

---

### 7. Forensic scientists must analyze evidence:
A) Quickly  
B) Without bias  
C) With police supervision  
D) In secrecy  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Without bias**  
*Explanation: Objectivity is essential to ensure accurate and scientifically valid conclusions.*
</details>
