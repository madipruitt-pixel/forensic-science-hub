### 114. Physical evidence acts as a:
A) Confession  
B) Thread connecting investigative findings  
C) Jury instruction  
D) Media statement  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**B) Thread connecting investigative findings**  
*Explanation: Physical evidence links people, places, and events, helping reconstruct what occurred.*
</details>

---

### 115. Physical evidence can help establish:
A) Timelines  
B) Locations  
C) Relationships  
D) All of the above  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**D) All of the above**  
*Explanation: Evidence can indicate when events happened, where they occurred, and connections between individuals.*
</details>

---

### 116. The presence of multiple evidence types generally:
A) Weakens a case  
B) Has no effect  
C) Increases probability of involvement  
D) Confuses investigators  

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
**C) Increases probability of involvement**  
*Explanation: Multiple types of corroborative evidence strengthen the link between suspect and crime scene.*
</details>
